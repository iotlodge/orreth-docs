Full auto in the Studio is one sentence. With the Studio open the chat speaks to its agent, and a
**brief** is the whole making:

```
make an agent called greeter that answers hello in three languages, with a golden case per language, on the opus stall
```

The wizard does not open — its word is the whole line *make an agent*, and a brief is longer. The
workshop agent thinks on the **opus** stall by stock (the binding names `claude-opus-5-5`; the MIND
chip or *assign workshop to …* still wins, and *unassign workshop* restores the stock), with twelve
rounds instead of the gateway's three, so it can make several crafts in one ask. Within a minute
the rail shows a bench **greeter** under YOUR BENCHES with its drafts under it, and the agent's
reply says in plain words what it made and that the Yes is yours.

## What it makes

The agent **plans the set**, **opens a bench**, calls its draft tool **once per craft** with the
whole declaration each time, and files **one held act**. On the brief above it made:

| Draft on the bench | What it is |
|---|---|
| **greeter.v0** — the graph | One think node pinned to the opus stall, with its prompt. |
| **greeter-words** — a prompt | The agent's own words for the body, made when the brief needs words of its own, and named on the think node. |
| **greeter** — the golden set | Three cases, one per language, each expecting short words the reply must contain (the harness's law). |
| **greeter** — the template | Kind agent, graph greeter.v0, a nature in one line, a mind naming opus's model. |

Every draft is yours to open and edit by hand on the rail before the Yes — or to let go with
*let the greeter bench go*.

## The one hold

The agent's last act is to file **put greeter to work** — the kernel's own hold, at L2, under
*your* seat, in your session, through the same door the bench's PUT TO WORK button uses. The
chat draws it as the agent's, because the held act's record carries who filed it:

> workshop made greeter on your brief · files put it to work · your Yes

with the chain's lines — *the prompt greeter-words as version 1 · the graph greeter.v0 as version
1 · the golden set greeter as version 1 · the agent greeter as version 1 → the experiment* — and
its own Yes and Cancel. The bench's card at PUT TO WORK holds the same ask with its second face.
Nothing of greeter stands on the shelf until you click. The tool tells the agent's mind *never
answer it*; a reading seat's brief is refused in words; a bench not whole refuses before any hold;
a second filing while the first waits refuses too.

## What it never does

The three modes law: **full auto is your dial of involvement, never the machine running alone, and a
consequence holds for a person in every mode.** The agent may never:

- **cut** — the cut is the chain's, on your Yes;
- **promote** — the promotion is a second, separate Yes;
- **seat** a life;
- **register** a source or a server;
- **answer a hold** — its own or any other.

Fully auto makes drafts and files one held act. It never passes the gate or the promotion by itself.
The walk's bar holds throughout: *it cut nothing, promoted nothing, seated nothing, registered
nothing, answered no hold.*

## Its hands

- **draft** — a draft filed on your open bench of that name (a bench not open refuses in words, so
  the agent opens it first).
- **bench** — open · read · work · close. *work* files the hold above; close lets the bench go.
- **the docs** — the two LangChain MCP servers on the shelf (`langchain-docs` and
  `langchain-reference`), their search and lookup tools named on the workshop agent's template and
  the librarian's. An MCP-born tool a template names is offered only while its server stands on
  the shelf, so a ground without the servers loses nothing and says nothing false.
- **the shelf's read**, and in its recalled notes THE STABLE (the stalls by name with their model)
  and YOUR BENCHES (your open benches and what stands on each, whole or not), under your seat alone.
