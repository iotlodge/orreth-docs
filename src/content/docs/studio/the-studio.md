**THE STUDIO** is the full-screen pull where an agent is made and tried before it is seated. It
was called the Workshop until four things were true — an agent's making has **one place** (the
bench); the canvas draws the **true graph** and lights it as a run goes; the ladder from draft to
experiment is **one word**; and the pull's own agent can **make it all** from a brief. All four
were proven by 2026-10-16, and the word turned with them. The machinery kept its names: the agent
that serves the pull is still the workspace body `workshop`, its binding is still
`bindings/workshop.v0.json`, and the chat hears **open the studio** and the older **open the
workshop** alike. This book says *the Studio* for the place and *the workshop agent* for the body
that answers you there.

The Studio stands on both kernels and ships in the published image `ghcr.io/iotlodge/orrethd:0.2.1`
of 2026-10-16 (the 0.1.0 image of 2026-09-30 predated it); the [quickstart](/build/quickstart/) lights
it, or run the kernel [from the repository](/build/from-the-repository/). The [register](/learn/what-works-today/)
says what of it is proven and what is still owed.

## The bench — one effort, one place

A **bench** is a named effort under your seat; the agent's name is the bench's name. Everything
about the agent's making is one row of the ground: its drafts, its step in the making, its
experiments, its lives (the trial lives and the seated ones), the hold it is waiting on, and its
wounds in plain words. Leave it, reload the page, change browsers, and it stands where you left it;
a second seat and the workshop agent see the same row.

The strip of steps on a bench reads **NAME · GRAPH · GOLDEN · SAVE · PUT TO WORK · THE CARD ·
PROMOTE · JOINED**. Every draft files *on* its bench; a draft with no bench is **loose**. A bench
closes by itself when its agent joins, and closes on your word when you let it go. Letting a bench
go is routine, like discarding a draft: its drafts are discarded, its open experiment declined,
and what reached the shelf stays — a version is never deleted.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| **new agent** · **make an agent** · **open the wizard** | A new bench with the wizard on it: name → graph → golden cases → SAVE → PUT TO WORK → the card → PROMOTE → joined. The same as **+ new agent** on the rail. |
| **open the jonathan bench** · **resume the jonathan bench** | Opens that bench where it stands. Its row on the rail does the same; typing an open bench's name at NAME opens it too. |
| **my benches** | Every bench with its step, its drafts and whether an experiment is open — *studio1 · at THE CARD · 0 drafts · an experiment open*. |
| **let the jonathan bench go** · **close the jonathan bench** | Routine: drafts discarded, the open experiment declined, the bench closed; what reached the shelf stands. |

The word *bench* has an older meaning in the kernel — the dispatcher's waiting bench where a
body's asks queue. The Studio's bench is a different table and the two never meet in code.

## The rails

The left rail reads, top to bottom: **YOUR BENCHES** first, each with its step and its drafts under
it; then **LOOSE DRAFTS**; then **THE SHELF** folded by kind with counts (*templates · 14*, open on a
click; retired crafts folded under their kind); then **THE HOUSE** — the crew's own crafts folded
under one row. The chat's residents rail shows the agents you made first, then *the house · N*
folded under one chip, with trial lives at rest folded too. The filter box finds anything by name
across every fold. The walk's first bar was *no scrolling zoo*.

## The hold wears two faces

A consequential act in the Studio — a cut, *put to work*, a promotion — is the kernel's one hold at
the interlock, and it shows in two places: pinned in the chat above the composer, and on the
bench's card with its own **Yes — do it** and **Cancel**. Either face answers the same hold through
the same door; the other settles the moment it does. The chat remains the record: every answer
lands there as a line whichever face took the click. A Yes is a click on either face, never Enter.

## The four panes and the chat

The Studio is four panes on one grid over the Panel's own: **THE SHELF** on the left, **THE CANVAS**
over **THE DECLARATION** in the middle, **THE INSPECTOR** on the right. Every seam is a splitter;
double-click one for the stock size; ⟲ layout puts every pane back. The chat floats over the
Studio as it does over the Panel; ⌖ pin on its header makes it the grid's own column. With the
Studio open the chat speaks to the workshop agent, and your open draft rides your ask so the agent
edits that draft on your words — it never cuts. The **MIND** chip in the header assigns the agent's
stall from the Stable, held at the interlock. Esc closes one thing at a time.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| **open the studio** · **studio** · **close the studio** | Opens or closes the Studio pull (one pull at a time; the STUDIO tab on the right does the same). The old words — **open the workshop** · **workshop** · **close the workshop** — are heard the same way. Opening pushes a focus: the chat speaks to the workshop agent until you close it. |
| **load the librarian template in the studio** · **load my bob draft in the studio** | Opens the Studio with that craft or draft loaded (*… in the workshop* is heard too). A craft from the shelf is read-only — *draft from this* copies it into a draft of yours. |
| **workshop, add an echo node after think** · **workshop, wire a way out of think to greet when the ask says hello** | The agent reads your open draft and edits it through its draft tool with the whole declaration; the canvas redraws and says so. |
| **(click a node)** | The node inspector: id · kind (think, echo, import) · prompt and skill · tools (narrowing, never past what the template declares) · mind · words. |
| **(click an arrow)** | The way out and its *when*: the default, the hull's edge (chat · work · event), a pattern in the ask, or a pattern in the last reply. |
| **SAVE** · **discard the draft** · **close the draft** · **clear the studio** | SAVE is routine, under your seat. Close keeps the draft and clears the canvas; only discard lets a draft go. |

## What stays the same

The kernel's one hold per consequential act; the Yes a click, never Enter; the chat as the record.
Drafts ordinary and under the person. The Experiment as the agent's gate with four reviewers under
their own names (how their verdicts and the kernel's five measurements relate is on
[the operating state](/learn/architecture/the-operating-state/)). Where the next life stands as the promotion's one field. The hull's laws never in
the agent's graph. The guide turning with the glass — say **guide** in the Studio and its section
opens (the guide is at 0.17.0 with the word turned).
