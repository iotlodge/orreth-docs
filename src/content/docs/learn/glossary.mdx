import glossary from '../../../data/glossary.json';

Orreth speaks a compact vocabulary, and the same word means the same thing on the panel, in the
chat, at the doors and in this book. Every definition below is plain, self-contained and under
240 characters — the contract the first architecture's console held its dictionary to, kept here
by hand for the new kernel until it serves a dictionary door of its own.

This book leads with plain engineering terms and shows the machine's names as labels — *"the
register of tools and services (the **shelf**)"*. Where a word below is capitalised, that is how
the glass writes it.
