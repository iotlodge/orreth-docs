Orreth's console speaks a compact working vocabulary. The definitions below
are **the machine's own**: they are served live by the running console (every
canon word there is a clickable definition), and the project's test suite
holds each one to a plain-English contract — self-contained, jargon-free, and
under 240 characters. This page and the console share one dictionary; when a
definition improves at the machine's editing gates, this page inherits it at
the next refresh.

## A note on the two vocabularies

These docs lead with plain engineering terms and show the machine's names as
labels — *"the tool registry (shown in the console as **the Farm**)"*. The
console keeps its own register because several of its words are load-bearing
distinctions with no shorter engineering equivalent (*saddled* is not merely
"configured"; *quarantined* is not merely "disabled"). When you meet a dotted
underline in the live console, it is this dictionary answering.
