Everything that reaches the kernel reaches it in one of a few declared ways, and the kernel says
which one it heard. This page is those ways side by side: how a **person** addresses the kernel;
how much of the person a task takes; how an **agent** is addressed; the **doors** themselves; and
**where a life stands**.

## 1 · How humans address the kernel — the kind of an ask

There are no chat modes. The chip under the composer reads the kind from your words — **thought ·
objective · intention** — and one click or one word flips it. The kind is minted as the ask's
marker, with the ask, in one transaction, so *the kernel read this as an objective; you confirmed*
is on the record.

| Kind | Say | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **thought** | *what is the temperature outside?* | One answer, now. Nothing in the world changes. |
| **objective** | *objective: get the weather tool healthy again* | A bounded outcome the crew owns until it is done: the planner writes the steps, the crew acts, the Analyzer shows the tree under its origin. |
| **intention** | *keep this world resilient: when a watch goes red, get it green* | A standing purpose above objectives, with no end and a stop. It wakes on what it is interested in — a red watch, a clock — plans the next objective itself and files it; the loop turns. |
| **a cadence** | *echo, every 5 seconds, say the time, please* | The smallest intention: cadence only. *echo, stop* rests it; *echo, restart intention 'every 5 seconds'* stands it again. |

An intention is a record: its words, what it serves (business · security · resiliency ·
compliance · cost), its kind (a human's, a role's, the kernel's), the marker kinds it is
interested in, its planner, its cadence, its gates, its stop. The kernel declares its own at boot
with no end and each with its stop — **Business · Security · Resiliency · Compliance · Cost** —
and, since the operating state landed, **THE OPERATING STATE**, which owns the watches on the five
measurements while Resiliency answers every other red. Saying the kernel's own words yourself is
refused with the reason: the kernel's is at work; stop it first, or say what is different.

The markers are the loop's vocabulary: an intention is a root, its objectives children, their acts
and observations under them; every fact wears its marker (why) beside its chain (who). The
[Analyzer](/watch/the-crew-and-the-analyzer/#the-analyzer-hatch) is that tree, by origin.

## 2 · The human's dial of involvement — manual · assist · full auto

The kind of your ask decides how far the kernel goes without you, and it never goes one step
further without a word from you. The three modes are **the human's dial**, never the machine running
alone.

| Mode | The ask | The kernel's part | Your part |
|---|---|---|---|
| **manual** | a thought | one body answers once | the question; you are the whole loop |
| **manual assist** | an objective | the planner writes the steps, the crew runs them; any step that would change something holds | one word to start, one word at each hold; *stop it* at any time, what was left undone is said |
| **full auto** | an intention, or a brief in the Studio | observes, reads the ground itself, plans, pulls a routine lever with its own hands, says what happened; you are told, not asked | declare once; decide holds when they come; rest it when you want |

One law holds the dial together: **a consequence holds for a person in every mode.** A routine act
runs at once; a **consequential** act holds at the interlock until a person clicks Yes, with Cancel
the default and the focus; a **grave** act asks for your authenticator's code and a declared
master's click. The kernel never lowers the ladder because it is running on its own. *Expected
impact?* on any hold asks the Master Mind In The Loop who and what the act touches first.

In the Studio, full auto is a brief: the workshop agent plans the set, drafts every craft on a
bench and files **one** held act, *put ‹name› to work*, for your single Yes. It never cuts,
promotes, seats, registers or answers a hold, and it never passes the gate or the promotion by
itself — the Experiment judges and the promotion is a second Yes. Three things always tell you
which mode a thing is in: the chip under the composer, the hold row, and the Analyzer with every
intention and objective by origin and its stop. [The agent makes it all](/studio/the-agent-makes-it-all/).

## 3 · How agents are addressed — the hull's three edges

Every body runs the hull — hear → recall → route → work → the reply — and at *route* the ask's
marker picks one of three edges. They are the three ways an agent is addressed, and the journey
says which one it took.

| Edge | Who is speaking | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **chat** | a human's words to a body | *echo, say the word HERON once*; *lang1, hello* from the bench; a brief to the workshop agent |
| **work** | assigned work — a duty on the clock, or an objective the intent loop filed | the librarian's hourly review; the kernel's harness run; *echo, every 5 seconds, say the time*; an objective's steps |
| **event** | a response to a marker the body declared interest in | the four reviewers asked under an experiment's marker; the critic under an *improvement*; Resiliency's planner under a *watch-red* |

A template declares the marker kinds it is *interested* in, and the kernel dispatches it an ask
whenever such a marker lands, with that marker as the parent. The chain says who; the marker says
why. [The hull](/learn/architecture/the-hull/) draws the ring.

## 4 · The doors

**The HTTP doors.** The Rust kernel `orrethd` is THE door on port 4600; it serves the one page and
every door of the world. A **seat** — the capability token the kernel signed after your code — is
read at every door, never the page; the unseated wear one face (`401 {"error": "not seated"}`), a
seated person lacking a grant wears the proof's (`403`), the browser origin is closed and a knock
ceiling stands per person. A body's door is its **lease**, the same shape of token, chained to the
kernel's root and opening the body's own words and nothing more. [The HTTP doors](/reference/http-api/).

**The feed.** The glass listens to one stream of small notices — a kind, an id, a reference — and
reads each fact through a door; the browser never touches a broker. The map counts the feed's
topics live.

**MCP servers, through one door.** *toolkeeper, add the MCP server at ‹command or URL›* lands a
server and its tools on the shelf as identities, each on the lifecycle ladder; its secrets ride by
env name only. A template names the tools a body may use (a node may narrow them further, never
widen), and an MCP-born tool a template names is offered only while its server stands on the shelf,
so a ground without the server loses nothing and says nothing false. The two LangChain docs servers
on the shelf are reached this way by the workshop agent and the librarian.

**The seam, between cells.** A universe is a cell — its own kernel self, database and role, sealed
from every other. Two cells that name each other speak over one door, the seam, in messages signed
by their kernels' own selves, the peer's DID pinned on first sight. *librarian@two, what is the
weather?* routes home and is answered here; under partition it parks in plain words and resumes
once. [How two worlds speak](/learn/how-the-kernel-works/#6--how-two-worlds-speak).

**Two kernels, one law.** The Python reference on port 4601 is the same kernel written first and
stays the yardstick: every law is pinned by a conformance fixture both kernels pass unchanged, and
the canonical bytes — sorted keys, compact separators, ASCII — are byte-identical across the SDK,
the reference and the kernel. The Studio's doors (the benches, the hops, the map) landed on both
kernels inside each spoonful, never after.

## 5 · Where a life stands — universe · floor · room

One kernel per cell serves three levels on one scope path: the **universe** (the cell itself), its
**floors**, and their **rooms** — `u:dev/f:ops/r:bob`. Each level is staffed: a crew can be seated on
any level by the manifest, what a body writes about itself wears its level, and a listing at a
level sees everything within it. Governance tightens down the path — a prod universe's floors and
rooms are prod, and *open room loose on floor three as dev* under a prod floor is refused in words.
Recall escalates up the path.

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| **what levels are here?** | Every floor and room with its mode, who is seated there, and what rolls up from within it. |
| **open floor ops** · **open room bob on floor ops as an experiment room** | Consequential, held at the interlock; a level stands forever. |
| **promote bob to floor ops** | The promotion's one field, **where the next life stands**: unsaid, where it stands now, else at the universe; an experiment room is refused. On the walk of 2026-10-16 the Yes seated greeter on floor ops. |
| **show the seats** | Every promoted agent: where its life stands, its template's version, its rung — active · rested · decommissioned. |

Named, not built: making a floor or a room *from* the promotion's card through the architect
firmware; **criticality and classification** as fields of every agent's template, read by the gate,
the reviewers and the promotion; a floor's own shelf, Stable and policy.
